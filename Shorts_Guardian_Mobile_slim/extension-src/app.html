MTRCB Rating Guide
What each age classification means in practice
G

General Audience · Ages 0–6

Safe for young children. We flag almost anything that isn't clearly kid-friendly — including fast vehicles, mild scares, or incidental alcohol in the background.

PG

Parental Guidance · Ages 7–13

Suitable with a parent nearby. We flag weapons, alcohol or tobacco, violence, bullying, romance, and similar mature themes.

SPG

Strong Parental Guidance · Ages 14+

For older teens. We only flag the most serious content — sexual content, self-harm, substances, weapons, and bullying.
